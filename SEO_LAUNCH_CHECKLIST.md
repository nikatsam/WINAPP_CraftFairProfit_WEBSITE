# SEO and Store Launch Checklist

The static site is structured for crawling; search engines decide whether and where to index or rank it. Do not promise ranking positions.

Owner decision: Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and Yandex Webmaster ownership verification/sitemap submission are deferred until the publisher chooses to resume SEO registration. No verification tokens are configured.

## After GitHub Pages is enabled

- Open each canonical URL on desktop and mobile and confirm images, navigation, calculator, guides, privacy page and 404 work.
- Submit `https://nikatsam.github.io/WINAPP_CraftFairProfit_WEBSITE/sitemap.xml` to Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and Yandex Webmaster after verifying ownership through each service.
- Inspect crawl/index coverage and canonical selection in each webmaster console. Re-submit after material URL/content changes.
- Add verified ownership tokens only when supplied by the actual account owner; none are fabricated in this repository.
- Use a custom domain only after the owner controls and verifies it; then update canonical URLs, sitemap, Open Graph URLs and webmaster properties consistently.

## Before Microsoft Store submission

- Verify that the public publisher name `Nikatsam` matches the Partner Center publisher identity; supply a private privacy/data-rights contact in `privacy.html` and review the policy against the final app build and Partner Center declarations.
- Replace the Store placeholder only after a real Microsoft Store listing is published and its canonical URL is known.
- Re-capture and review genuine screenshots from the release build; current site screenshots are from the demo workspace.
- Recheck all product claims, Windows requirements, privacy statements, copyright and locale copy.

## Editorial safeguards

- Keep scenarios labelled illustrative; do not fabricate reviews, customer testimonials, market statistics, income outcomes or search-volume data.
- Update guide facts when app behavior changes. Keep `SERP_RESEARCH.md` qualitative until reliable query/volume data is available.
- Use concise unique titles/descriptions, one descriptive H1, useful internal links, descriptive alt text, valid structured data matching visible text, and a current sitemap.
- Do not add doorway pages, keyword stuffing, hidden content or structured data for claims not visible on the page.
